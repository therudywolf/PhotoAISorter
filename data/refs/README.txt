Reference photos for fast (CLIP) sorting
=====================================

Put a few clear photos in each subfolder (5–20 JPEG/PNG per tag):

  refs/iam/          — photos of you (any angle)
  refs/iam_face/     — face close-ups (optional)
  refs/my_dog/       — your pet
  refs/my_cat/       — your pet

Only folders that exist are used. Tag definitions live in tmp/app_state/context_tags.json
(see examples/context_tags.example.json).
